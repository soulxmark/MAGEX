Behind the Lens

I am a visual storyteller and cinematographer specializing in capturing the raw energy of events through professional photography and high-end videography. From cinematic portraits to fast-paced Same Day Edit (SDE) productions, my goal is to turn fleeting moments into lasting legacies.

My approach blends technical precision with creative intuition. By utilizing industry-standard tools like the Sony Alpha series and DJI stabilization systems, I ensure every frame is polished, stabilized, and color-graded to a professional standard.

When I'm not on a shoot or in the editing suite refining node trees in DaVinci Resolve, I’m likely exploring new perspectives or integrating my background in Information Systems to streamline creative workflows.
